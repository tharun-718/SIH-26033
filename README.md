## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1. Develop a digital platform to connect farmers directly with consumers.
2. Allow farmers to sell products without middlemen.
3. Connect farmers with shops, hotels and bulk buyers.
4. Provide delivery and transportation support.
5. Use AI to predict demand and suggest suitable prices.

## Technical Approach
1.Frontend: HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
2.Backend: Python.
3.Database: MySQL.
4.AI: Machine Learning for demand prediction.
5.Maps: Google Maps API for route optimization.

## Feasibility and Viability
1.The platform can be developed using affordable technologies.
2.Farmers can easily register and upload their products.
3.Internet access is required to use the platform.
4.Transportation and product quality are major challenges.
5.We can overcome these challenges through local delivery partners and quality checks.

## Impact and Benefits
1.Farmers can earn more profit.
2.Consumers can buy products at lower prices.
3.Middlemen can be reduced.
4.Food wastage can be minimized.
5.Rural farmers can access a wider market.
6.Transportation costs can be reduced through route optimization.
